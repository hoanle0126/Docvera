## Design Context

### Users
Self-hosting operators and their end users who need to process PDFs and documents privately: merge, convert, OCR, redact. They come to finish one concrete file task quickly and to trust that their files stay on the server.

### Brand Personality
Precise, archival, accountable. It should feel like a well-kept records office: honest status, no hype, no false-green health checks.

### Aesthetic Direction
Type: Archivo (bundled, OFL) with its width axis carrying hierarchy (wide, heavy display; normal-width UI), and JetBrains Mono only for literal code, file extensions and IDs. A paper canvas (#f5f3ee), ink (#141413) and one vermilion accent (#c23b22); metadata uses small, wide, tracked caps instead of mono. Use hairline rules, square corners (2-4px) and ledgers (one ruled line per item) rather than card grids. Status is a small square marker plus a word, never a colored chip. Light theme, because this is daytime office work.
Avoid: glassmorphism, gradients, pill badges, side-stripe accent borders, SaaS hero templates and generic drop shadows.

### Design Principles
1. Structure carries identity: rules, ledgers, stamps and index labels, not decoration.
2. State is labelled in words (a small caps label), never only by a colored stripe.
3. Labels, badges and nav items never wrap. Long text truncates or wraps around them.
4. The header is one ruled line. On small screens secondary items move into a menu instead of a second row.
5. Copy uses sentence case, says "and" instead of "&", and makes every word earn its place.
